# Fuse, 3-minute video script

Voiceover, plain and unhurried. Cut the model wait times.

## 0:00 Hook (15 s)
This is Fuse, built for the iPhone Duo. You put one thing on each half of the phone. Then you fold it. Folding is the command: Fuse reads both screens and makes the one thing you actually needed from them.

## 0:15 How it works (25 s)
Each half runs a live app: a browser, maps, notes, photos, files, your calendar. When the hinge closes, we use Apple's new onHingeChange API to catch the fold, capture both screens as text and pixels, and send them to OpenAI's gpt-6 model with one question: what is the relationship between these two things, and what should exist because of it. It answers with a typed artifact, an itinerary, an event, a grade, a comparison, an email, or a brand new image, and the result lands on the cover display while the phone is still closed.

## 0:40 Theme park (25 s)
The Islands of Adventure page on the left, the park pinned in Maps on the right. Before I fold, the seam already tells me what it will do. Fold. Open it back up: a timed day plan, pinned on the map, in the order you'd actually walk it. One tap adds every stop to my calendar.

## 1:05 Two photos, one image (25 s)
A photo on each half and a sentence. Fold. Fuse hands both pictures to the image model and gets back a single new image with both people in it. That's a picture that didn't exist thirty seconds ago.

## 1:30 Conference and calendar (25 s)
A conference email on one side, my real week on the other. Fold. It found the slot, wrote the event, and flagged the two things it collides with. Add to Calendar is a real button; it's in my calendar now.

## 1:55 Shopping (20 s)
Two Amazon product pages, side by side, the way you'd actually compare. Fold. Price, range, speed, weight, hills, and a verdict for my commute.

## 2:15 Studying (25 s)
A practice test on the left, my answers on the right. Fold. It grades me, shows exactly where I went wrong, and names my weak spots. Then I tap one follow-up and it writes a new quiz on just those, and I can take it right here. Results chain; every answer can become the next input.

## 2:40 Split the bill, and close (20 s)
The receipt, and who had what. Fold. Everyone's share, tax and tip included.
Fuse is free to use. RevenueCat powers Fuse Pro for image fusion, Supabase keeps a shared feed of fuses, and everything you saw runs on the Duo simulator today. The hinge isn't navigation. It's the command.
