# Fuse — demo script (3:00)

Recorded in the Bitrig iPhone Duo simulator, 3D view. You can grab the phone with the cursor and fold it; hold Option to get the hinge slider if you want a precise angle. Keep the device in the open, book pose for compose and close it fully for each fuse. Dark room, one take per scenario, cut between.

Before recording: keys pasted in Settings, RevenueCat quota reset so the paywall appears on cue, the calendar surface showing its sample week, network warm (run one fuse and discard it).

Notation: **SAY** is voice-over, **DO** is what happens on screen.

---

## 0:00 – 0:20 — Hook

**DO** Open Duo, flat. Left pane: the Islands of Adventure article. Right pane: a map pin on the park. No narration for the first two seconds; let the two screens read.

**SAY** "Two screens. Two things. On every foldable ever made, this is where it ends: a bigger screen, or two apps side by side."

**DO** Grab the phone and fold it in one motion. The panes melt toward the seam as the angle drops. Phone closes. Cover display shows the itinerary card: "Islands in one day."

**SAY** "On Fuse, the fold is the command."

---

## 0:20 – 1:05 — Theme park + map → itinerary

**DO** Reopen the phone. The itinerary lays out across both screens: stops with times on the left, the map with numbered pins on the right.

**SAY** "I put the park's page on one side and a pin on the other. I did not pick a feature. Fuse read both screens, saw a place and a map, and decided the useful thing was a plan."

**DO** Scroll the stops slowly. Point at a specific one.

**SAY** "It is specific because the screens were specific. Real ride names from the article. Real coordinates from the pin. Hagrid's first, before the line builds."

**DO** Tap a stop; the map recenters on it.

**SAY** "Nothing here is a template. The relationship between the two screens is the feature."

---

## 1:05 – 1:45 — Conference email + calendar → event with conflicts

**DO** Tap the wordmark to open the demo menu, tap "Fold it into my week." Left pane fills with the Swiftsonic confirmation email. Right pane shows the calendar week.

**SAY** "An email about a conference, next to my week. Fold."

**DO** Fold to closed. Cover shows the event card. Reopen: event on the left with title, dates, Music City Center; on the right the calendar with the new block and a conflict warning badge.

**SAY** "It pulled the dates, the venue, and my speaking slot out of the email. It also checked the other screen. That is my week, so it tells me what I am double-booked with and how far the hotel is from the venue."

**DO** Tap "Add to Calendar." The event writes through EventKit; a small confirmation appears.

**SAY** "One fold. One event. Conflicts included."

---

## 1:45 – 2:25 — Test + answers → grade → follow-up quiz

**DO** Demo menu, tap "Grade my practice test." Left: the six-question physics test with its answer key. Right: Maya's handwritten-style answers.

**SAY** "A practice test with the key on one side. A student's answers on the other."

**DO** Fold. Reopen. Grade report: 4/6, each question marked, two wrong with feedback, weak spots listed: kinematics round-trip time, spring energy formula.

**SAY** "Four out of six. It shows the two misses, why they are wrong, and names the two concepts underneath them."

**DO** Point at the follow-up chips at the bottom. Tap "Make a practice test on my weak spots."

**SAY** "Every result suggests the next fuse. Same two screens, new instruction."

**DO** The melt plays again without folding; a five-question quiz appears, all about projectile round-trips and elastic potential energy. Tap one choice; it marks and explains.

**SAY** "It generated a quiz aimed exactly at what she got wrong. No re-typing, no re-folding."

---

## 2:25 – 2:50 — Inputs, Pro, community

**DO** Back to compose with two surfaces loaded. Pinch the two halves together with two fingers; the melt follows the pinch and fires at the end.

**SAY** "You do not have to fold. Pinch the halves together."

**DO** Compose again. Press and hold the seam core; the ramp fills. Release.

**SAY** "Or hold the seam."

**DO** Compose. Hold the mic, say "Turn this into a checklist," release. The instruction chip appears; the fuse fires.

**SAY** "Or tell it what you want while you fold."

**DO** Show the phone closed. Double-tap the back. It fuses.

**SAY** "Or bind Fuse to Back Tap and fuse a closed phone."

**DO** Trigger one more fuse; the RevenueCat paywall slides up: Fuse Pro, monthly, restore.

**SAY** "Fuse Pro through RevenueCat. Free fuses every day, unlimited on Pro."

**DO** Dismiss. Tap the community icon. The Supabase feed scrolls: other people's fuses, titles and recipes, tap one to open.

**SAY** "Every public fuse lands in a shared feed on Supabase, so you can see what pairs other people found."

---

## 2:50 – 3:00 — Close

**DO** Back to compose, two screens loaded. Hold the shot on the open phone for a beat, then fold it closed. Cut to black on the click.

**SAY** "The hinge is not navigation. It is the command. Fuse."

---

## Fallbacks

- If a live fuse is slow, cut to a pre-recorded result and keep the voice-over; the melt is the beat that matters.
- If the web surface fails to load, the demo menu re-applies the scenario; the Wikipedia URLs are stable.
- If the hinge simulator is not responding, hold Option and use the slider; the melt is identical.
- Keep every fold under one second on camera. The result should appear while the phone is still closing.
